The most realistic department

The Buford County Sheriff's Office is recognized as one of the most realistic and immersive law enforcement departments on the server. The department is led by Sheriff Daniel Aldric, a former Sergeant First Class who served his home county for five years before retiring to pursue a doctoral degree in Criminal Justice.

BCSO operates a comprehensive three-phase academy program built on realism, accountability, and professional development — creating an authentic experience that reflects the standards of a real-world sheriff's office.

What we stand for

Four values guide every Deputy's conduct, on duty and off.

I

Integrity

Doing the right thing even when no one is watching, and holding ourselves to the same standard we hold others.

E

Excellence

Training hard, documenting thoroughly, and treating every call as an opportunity to do the job right.

F

Fairness

Enforcing the law consistently, without bias, for every resident of Buford County.

R

Respect

Treating the public, and each other, the way we'd want our own families treated.

"Our mission at the Buford County Sheriff's Office is to serve and protect the people of Buford County, providing the highest quality experience through the dedication of our Deputies. As Sheriff, I am committed to preserving the integrity of BCSO and embodying these values in everything we do."
— Sheriff Daniel Aldric, Buford County Sheriff
