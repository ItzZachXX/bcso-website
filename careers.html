Careers at BCSO

Serve your community. Build your future.

From the front line to specialized divisions, the Buford County Sheriff's Office offers a structured path for anyone ready to train hard and serve well.

Why choose BCSO

A department built on realism and mentorship, not just a badge.

1

Real training

A structured three-phase Academy, Field Training, and Patrol Observation program, not a rubber stamp.

2

Clear advancement

Defined ranks and specialized divisions with a visible path from Deputy to Command Staff.

3

Mentorship

Every Probationary Deputy is paired with a certified Training Instructor from day one.

4

Camaraderie

A department that trains, patrols, and grows together, on shift and off.

Career opportunities

Current openings across patrol, corrections, and support roles.

FULL TIME

Deputy Sheriff

Step into the front lines of law enforcement, protecting Buford County while building lifelong camaraderie with fellow Deputies. Your gateway to specialized divisions.

Entry point · No experience required
FULL TIME

Sheriff Corporal

Take the next step in leadership by guiding Deputies, fostering teamwork, and making a lasting impact while serving your community.

Requires Deputy tenure
PART TIME

Corrections Deputy

Join the team at Buford County Penitentiary, ensuring safety and security in one of the most challenging and rewarding roles in law enforcement.

Open to part- & full-time Deputies
PART TIME

Civil Matters

Be the presence of order in the courtroom, ensuring safety during trials and proceedings while representing the strength and integrity of BCSO.

Part-time role for full-time Deputies
PART TIME

Field Training Officer

Shape the future of BCSO by mentoring and training the next generation of Deputies, passing on your knowledge in this vital leadership role.

Available to part- & full-time Deputies
SELF-PACED

Academy Phase

Every application begins with the Academy Phase: remote instruction on SOPs, penal codes, radio procedure, and legal foundations, followed by a written exam.

Required before Phase II
