How BCSO is organized

Every Deputy reports through a clear chain of command, from Probationary Deputy up through Command Staff. Rank is earned through time in service, demonstrated competence, and adherence to the standards laid out in the Field Training & Standard Operating Procedures Handbook.

Command Staff sets policy, reviews commendations and complaints, and signs off on every Deputy's release from probationary status after they complete the Academy, Field Training, and Patrol Observation phases.
